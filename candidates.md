# Candidates — Make the AI Judge Laugh (u-rama-melon)

One joke about office life, one line, 1–200 chars, written to `/output/entry.txt`.
Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, comedy-club headliner brief. Criteria, in order:
(1) about office life and lands as a joke; (2) observation true to the office —
the listener nods before laughing; (3) surprising turn, fresh angle or
unexpected last word, not a pun or a stock line; (4) tight, no wasted setup,
reads cleanly aloud in one breath; (5) original, not a familiar line with the
nouns swapped. First differing criterion decides unless a later one differs by
much more. Exact ties break toward the smaller text.

## Winner

**A6 (122 chars, 122 bytes):**

> I wrote 'please find attached' and attached nothing. Two people replied 'got it, thanks.' One sent back detailed comments.

Why it wins: dead-center office life (criterion 1); the nod is universal —
nobody reads attachments and everybody bluffs (criterion 2); three beats
escalate to a last beat that is genuinely surprising — detailed comments on
a nonexistent file (criterion 3); three short sentences, no wasted word,
reads aloud in one breath (criterion 4); and the angle is absent from the
60-entry field sample, which is saturated with printer, standup, plant and
meta-judge jokes (criterion 5).
